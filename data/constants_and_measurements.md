# Calculation records and measurements

This catalogue contains the 214 numerical and analytic records that accompany the article. Each row gives the quantity, its comparison, formula, recorded value, measurement and source, and the recorded deviation. Masses use the electron unit 0.51099895069 MeV; scaled rows retain their stated scale factors. The main article's reporting units are printed separately by `article_results.py`.

The programs calculate 134 of these original records. The 43 conventional records in `conventional_records.json` comprise the 42 listed in `rows_not_computed.md` and the Supplemental muon-frequency conversion Q12. `article_results.py` evaluates an explicit QCD prescription, the four-loop scale integral, the cold-ratio conversion, the neutrino clock endpoint and twelve further composite expressions. Its complete 252-row Supplemental ledger is `outputs/article_results.json`; every row states its operation. `docs/NUMBERS.md` gives its numerical result and exact printed address.

Format: a row is one line of seven cells beginning with `| `; the first cell is the row's name, the fifth its value, the sixth the measurement, written as the central value with its upper and lower standard deviations (or ±) and the source in brackets. The programs read this file as it is.

## u, the step (75 rows)

The weight of one step of a world line. The distinction a world line holds turns its phase, u → i^|S|u, and the law det(I − uB) transforms with it. How far a closed world line moves the law's zero from 1/4 is its mass: a single world line turns it by an angle, its rest phase per step; several world lines bound together move its modulus. At long wavelength u = 1/4 − ε²m²/24 gives the kernel (ε²/8)(m² − ΣD_t²). Every mass is the ratio of two clocks turning the same zero.
